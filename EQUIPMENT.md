# Equipment list

This is the kit the booth was built and tested with. Nothing here is required by name: the program only needs a camera the browser can see, a tablet with a browser, and a laptop. Items marked **swap** have an easy substitute. Items marked **optional** you can leave out.

How it fits together: the graduate stands about 52 cm from the lens and writes on a clear acrylic pane held about 22 cm in front of the lens. The camera films through the pane, so the writing is seen from behind and the program flips the clip so it reads correctly. A tablet beside the left stand, outside the camera's view, shows the steps.

## Camera and video

| Item | Model used | What it does in the rig |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | Sony ZV-E10 with the 16-50 mm power-zoom kit lens, set to 16 mm, movie mode | Films the graduate through the pane. Sends a clean picture over HDMI. **Swap:** any camera with clean HDMI out, or a good webcam. |
| Camera power | KingMa NP-FW50 dummy battery (USB-C powered, model TC-NPFW50), run from a 20 W or stronger USB-C charger | Keeps the camera on mains power so it cannot die mid-event. A real Sony NP-FW50 battery kept as a spare. **Optional** if the camera is on a charged battery for a short session. |
| Capture card | UGREEN 4K60Hz USB-C HDMI capture card (1080p60, USB 3.0) | Turns the camera's HDMI into a normal camera the laptop's browser can use. **Swap:** Elgato Cam Link 4K (needs a USB-C to USB-A adapter on a laptop with only USB-C). Avoid USB 2.0 dongles: they squeeze the picture. |
| HDMI cable | Vention micro-HDMI to HDMI 2.0 cable, 3 m | Camera to capture card. Micro-HDMI at the camera end means no adapters. Tape it to the stand so a pull does not reach the camera port. |
| Backup video path | The camera's own USB-C port in USB streaming mode, plus a USB-C data cable | Plan B if the capture card fails. Gives 720p, so it is a fallback only. **Optional.** |
| Tripod | Manfrotto 190XPROB with a 496RC2 ball head | Holds the camera at the height of the graduate's face. |

Camera settings that mattered: HDMI resolution 1080p, HDMI info display off, auto power-off temperature set to high, rear screen flipped away so it does not show on the glass.

## Pane, stands and arms

| Item | Model used | What it does in the rig |
|---|---|---|
| Clear acrylic pane | 30 x 40 cm, 4 mm thick, held in landscape, cut with polished edges | The surface people write on. A small pane close to the lens fills the frame and avoids a large, heavy glass sheet. 6 mm is stiffer if you can afford the weight. |
| Light stands | 2 x Meking MF-3400F 3.4 m combo stands | Carry the arms that hold the pane and the tablet. |
| Super clamps | Meking M11-034 super clamps, about 8 in total | Fix the arms to the stands and the pane grips to the arms. |
| Magic arms | 4 x Meking MB-42 heavy-duty magic arms, 28 cm | Three hold the pane (three grips that do not sit in a line keep it from rocking), one holds the tablet. |
| 1/4-20 studs | SRP-102 or similar | Thread adapters where an arm meets a clamp. |
| Foam pads | 5 mm EVA foam | Cushion the pane edges where the grips touch, so the acrylic does not crack or slip. |
| Sandbags | 8 x 5 kg | Weigh down the stand feet. Do not run a stand without them. |

## Tablet

| Item | Model used | What it does in the rig |
|---|---|---|
| Tablet | Samsung Galaxy Tab S8 Ultra (14.6 in), landscape | Shows the steps, countdown, recording and QR. **Swap:** any tablet that can open a web page; a smaller one is fine. |
| Tablet mount | SmallRig 2930 tablet mount | Holds the tablet on a magic arm beside the left stand, outside the camera's view and about 6.5 cm behind the glass plane so the graduate can reach it. |
| Wi-Fi | The tablet's own mobile hotspot | The laptop joins it, so the laptop and tablet are on the same network without venue Wi-Fi. **Swap:** any shared Wi-Fi. |

## Laptop and storage

| Item | Model used | What it does in the rig |
|---|---|---|
| Laptop | MacBook Pro 13 in (2017, Touch Bar, four USB-C ports) | Runs the booth: records, saves, serves the share page. Four USB-C ports means no hub: the capture card, charger, USB stick and a spare all plug straight in. **Swap:** a Linux laptop with rclone for the Google Drive copy (see the README). |
| USB-C hub with power pass-through | Any, 60 W or more | **Optional.** Only for a laptop with fewer ports. |
| Backup stick | USB-C stick, 32 GB or more, formatted exFAT, named `BOOTH-USB` | The booth can write a second copy of each clip here. **Optional.** |
| Spare cable | 2 m USB-C data cable | For the Plan B video path and for charging. |

## Light and background

| Item | Model used | What it does in the rig |
|---|---|---|
| Key light | amaran (Aputure) P60c with softbox, grid and mains supply, on a light stand | Soft light on the person, aimed at them and off the pane. Mains supply, not battery. One is enough if you add a fill. |
| Fill reflector | White foam board, opposite the light | **Optional.** Bounces light back onto the shadow side of the face. |
| Black backdrop | Selens black velvet cloth, 1.5 x 1 m (non-reflective) | Hung behind the camera so nothing bright reflects in the pane. This did more than any other fix. |
| Hood | A DIY card or foam box around the lens, gaffer tape | Stops side light hitting the pane. |
| Polariser | 40.5 mm clip-on CPL filter | **Optional.** Was tried and dropped; the backdrop and hood worked better. |

## Writing and cleaning

| Item | What it does in the rig |
|---|---|
| Markers | Window or dry-erase markers in two or three colours. Test them on the pane first: some leave a film. |
| Glass cleaner (ammonia-free) | Ammonia clouds acrylic. |
| Microfibre cloths, a small squeegee | Wipe the pane between people. |
| Wet wipes and tissues | For hands and spills. |

## Power, tape and odds and ends

- Extension lead or multi-plug (for the laptop, camera power and light).
- Gaffer tape for cables, masking or floor tape to mark where the graduate stands (about 52 cm from the lens).
- Power bank. **Optional**, a short-term fallback for the tablet.

## Lessons from using this kit

- A pane close to the lens is much easier to light and frame than a large one far away. Keep the person about 50 cm back so their face and the writing are both sharp.
- Reflections are the main enemy: black behind the camera, light aimed at the person, hood on the lens.
- Check the capture card says USB 3.0 on the box and on the laptop's port.
- Always do two test clips with real markers before the event.
